About Grace

NYC-based braider passionate about creating distinctive, athletic-grade, and celebratory hair styling.

Welcome to Braid My Heart! I specialize in precision braided styles, vibrant feed-ins, and one-of-a-kind ribbon braids designed for athletes, children, and special celebrations throughout New York City.

One of my signature specialties is race-day styling—including the iconic Victory Ribbon Braids featured at the NYC Marathon. These styles are designed to stay locked in place through all 26.2 miles, giving runners high-visibility pops of color that help friends and family spot them along the racecourse.

Beyond race and festival styling, I love working with young clients (our "Mini Braids") and hosting interactive braiding workshops. Whether you're a parent wanting to learn daily hair routines with your child or looking to master intricate 4- and 5-strand ribbon techniques, our workshops provide hands-on, supportive instruction.
